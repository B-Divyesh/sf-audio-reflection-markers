Policy Privacy

Privacy

Your markers and recordings stay in this browser unless you export a backup.

What is stored

Markers, source references, recall history, optional action dates, and voice notes are stored in this browser. The current link can stay available after a refresh.

What is not collected

The app has no account, analytics, uploads, transcription, or remote AI processing. Local media files are opened for this session and are not added to your marker list.

Microphone and external links

Microphone permission is requested only after you press Record. A source link opens its original service, which has its own privacy policy.

Your control

Use Data and settings to export a complete JSON backup or delete every marker. Clearing this site’s browser data also erases the local database. Export first if you need to preserve it.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the project repository linked from the product’s deployment record. Effective 28 August 2026.
